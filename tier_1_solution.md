# Tier 1 -- Basic Understanding: The Literal Assistant

## Challenge: Make Me Breakfast

### 1. Assumptions and choices

The original instruction, "Make me breakfast," does not say what food to prepare or how to prepare it. To make the task executable
without guessing, this solution uses the following specific choices:

* Prepare two scrambled eggs and two slices of toasted bread.
* Use the eggs and bread stored in the refrigerator and kitchen cupboard.
* Prepare the food in the kitchen.
* Serve the breakfast on one clean plate.
* Place the plate on the kitchen table.
* Do not begin if an ingredient or required piece of equipment is missing.


These are example choices. A real assistant should confirm them with the person before starting if their preferences are unknown.

### 2. Equipment and ingredients
Ingredients - 2 eggs - 2 slices of bread - 1 teaspoon of cooking oil - A small pinch of salt

Equipment - 1 frying pan - 1 cooking spoon or spatula - 1 toaster -1 plate - 1 fork - 1 small bowl - 1 teaspoon - A stove

### 3. Precise step-by-step instructions

* Go to the kitchen.
* Wash your hands with soap and water, then dry them.
* Check that all the listed ingredients and equipment are available.
* Take 2 eggs from the refrigerator.
* Take 2 slices of bread from the kitchen cupboard.
* Place the frying pan on the stove.
* Turn on the stove to low heat.
* Measure 1 teaspoon of cooking oil and pour it into the frying pan.
* Break both eggs into the small bowl. Check that no eggshell pieces fall into the bowl.
* Add a small pinch of salt to the eggs.
* Use the fork to beat the eggs until the yolks and whites are mixed.
* Pour the beaten eggs into the heated frying pan.
* Use the spatula to stir the eggs slowly while they cook.
* Continue cooking until the eggs are fully set, with no runny liquid remaining. 
* Do not leave the pan unattended.
* Turn off the stove.
* Put the 2 slices of bread into the toaster.
* Toast the bread until it is lightly golden. If the toaster has a setting, use a medium setting.
* Remove the toast carefully.
* Place the scrambled eggs and the 2 slices of toast on the clean plate.
* Place the plate on the kitchen table.
* Make sure the stove is switched off and the cooking area is safe.
* Clean and put away the equipment after it has cooled, and wash any used dishes.
* Tell the person that breakfast is ready.

### 4. How to know the task is finished
The task is complete when all of the following conditions are true:

* Two scrambled eggs have been cooked until fully set.
* Two slices of bread have been toasted until lightly golden.
* Both foods are on one clean plate.
* The plate is on the kitchen table.
* The stove is switched off.
* The cooking area has been left safe and clean.
* The person has been told that breakfast is ready.

### 5. Reasoning challenge: What assumptions were missing?
The original instruction, "Make me breakfast," required several
assumptions:

* Food choice: It did not say what breakfast food to prepare.
* Quantity: It did not say how much food was needed.
* Location: It did not say where the ingredients or equipment
were.
* Ingredients: It did not list the ingredients to use.
* Equipment: It did not identify the tools or appliances needed.
* Order of actions: It did not explain which task should happen first or next.
* Cooking method: It did not explain how the food should be prepared.
* Safety: It did not explain how to handle the stove or hot equipment safely.
* Serving location: It did not say where to put the finished breakfast.
* Completion: It did not explain how to know when breakfast was ready.

### 6. Key lesson
* Humans often understand incomplete instructions because they use experience and context to fill in missing details.

* Computers and literal machines cannot reliably do this. They need clear information, specific steps, and conditions that tell them when a task
is complete.

Conclusion: A vague instruction such as "Make me breakfast" must be broken down into precise, ordered instructions before a computer can
execute it reliably.
